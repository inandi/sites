Water Boy is a hydration reminder Android application designed to help you track your daily water consumption. It features beautiful 3D water animations, smart periodic reminders, comprehensive statistics, and a modern Material 3 design interface.

Simply tap the drink button on the main screen to log your water consumption. Each tap records one glass of water based on your configured glass size. The app tracks your daily progress in real-time and stores all your data locally on your device.

The app features realistic 3D water visualization with physics-based effects. The water responds to your device movement (tilt effect), includes a stunning particle system with bubbles, sparkles, and ripples, and provides smooth wave animations and glass effects that make tracking your hydration visually engaging.

Water Boy sends automatic reminders every 3 hours from 9 AM to 9 PM (at 9 AM, 12 PM, 3 PM, 6 PM, and 9 PM). Each notification includes quick action buttons (Yes/No) to log water directly from the notification. Notifications automatically dismiss after 15 minutes to prevent them from piling up. The app uses your device's default notification sound.

No, Water Boy does not currently sync data across devices. All data is stored locally on each device. This ensures your privacy and allows the app to work completely offline. If you use multiple devices, you'll need to track separately on each one.

If you uninstall Water Boy, all your local data (water records, settings, statistics) will be permanently deleted. Since all data is stored locally on your device, there's no cloud backup to restore from. Make sure you're certain before uninstalling if you want to keep your data.
